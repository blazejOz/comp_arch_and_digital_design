NASM (intel syntax)

compiling:
nasm -f elf32 math.asm -o prog.o && gcc -m32 prog.o -o prog.out && ./prog.out

INSTRUCTION DESTINATION, SOURCE
mov eax, 27 - move value 27 into eax register

General Purpose Registers

EAX - accumulator, used to automatically in multiplication and division
EBX - base register
ECX - counter, often used in loops
EDX - data, holds overflow//remainder in math operations
